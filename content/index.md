# Yijung Knowledge Vault

## Overview

Welcome, reader! This page is your guide to the DnD Campaign Setting Multiverse, known as [[Yijung]], generated by the meditations of the [[Khamdak Gyurmé|Source of All]].  Yijung is filled with multitudinous *[[Locations MOC#Planes|Planes]]*, or Universes. There are three main things that tie them together:
1. Their source is the meditations of a monk named [[Khamdak Gyurmé]], known by a rare few on [[Salbé]] as The [[Khamdak Gyurmé|Omnisemdrang]]. 
2. In Yijung; Buddhism is true; laws of physics and magic may change from plane to plane but Dharma is always true. 
3. There is no backwards time travel. Planes may experience time at different rates that may fluctuate relative to each other but they ***all*** flow in the same direction.
