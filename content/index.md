# Yijung Knowledge Vault
## Overview
Welcome, reader! This page is your guide to the DnD Campaign Setting Multiverse, known as [[Yijung]], generated by the meditations of the [[Khamdak Gyurmé|Source of All]].  Yijung is filled with multitudinous *[[Multiverse MOC#Planes|Planes]]*, or Universes. There are three main things that tie them together:
1. Their source is the meditations of a monk named [[Khamdak Gyurmé]], known by a rare few on [[Salbé]] as The [[Khamdak Gyurmé|Omnisemdrang]]. 
2. In Yijung; Buddhism is true; laws of physics and magic may change from plane to plane but Dharma is always true.
