## Summary
Has lost most of its influence with [[Creation of the Frostlands]] being cut off from the Southern Continent and source of many of its soldiers. It now controls the pass between the main mountain ranges of [[Yolsa]] and out to [[Mount Gemmarca]]. Otherwise has large amounts of influence over they [[Goblin]], who are just a protectorate, and [[Goliath Tribes]] which are still part of the [[Imperium Jampyul|The Imperium]] but very loosely affiliated.
