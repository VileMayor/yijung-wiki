## Stylized Appearance

Shown most often in blood splattered plate with horns raising from the head. It is a masculine figure that is 9 foot tall.  He rest a hand on the hilt of a wicked sword ranks 8 inches into the ground. His armor is intricately carved and his helm obscures his face but eyes glow red with in.
