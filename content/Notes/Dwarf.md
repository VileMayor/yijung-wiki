## Short Description
A race constructed by [[Schmiedevater]] that is short, bearded, and stocky. They only have one sex and lay eggs. Incredibly formal and bureaucratic

## Biology & Physiology
### Reproduction
The dwarves lay eggs and may produce sexually or asexually. There are different cultural names and roles for offspring for the children depending on conception method.

### Society
### Divisions based on Conception Method
#### Herzenskind (Heart-Child)
Born from a union of two dwarves these are the most common dwarves.
#### Sprössling (Sprout/Offspring)
Born asexual, a clone of the parent. Uncommon. A little taboo. Something some of the clergy may choose to do. They are celibate. 
#### Sterben-sprössling (Sprout of Dying)
Coming into vogue 400 years ago after a shake up in dwarven society when a lot of houses fortune's changed. And one of the most powerful new money leader's on his death bed produced a Sprössling. He mandated the Sprössling be trained all the classical dwarven instruments and set up trust fund and build him a concert hall to perform in. 

This trend has continued with powerful dwarves producing eggs on their deathbeds. Setting up trust funds for them and mandating that they live the lives the dying never got to.
