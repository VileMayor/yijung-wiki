# [[Damtsig-pa]]
---
>The Commited || Tibetan | Literally "Vow One"
---
## Overview
Magic users that gain their power through interaction with a deity. Like the [[Nüpé-tönpa]] they technically manipulate the [[Fundamental Energies]] but do so via an intermediary. They do not need to understand how this is done just the system created by the one who grants them power. 

---

## Classes
- [[Cleric]] - Gains power through devotion to deities. 
- [[Druid]] - Gains power through reverence and guardianship of nature. At time still have a deity or spirits. 
- [[Paladin]] - Bound to deity by sacred vow. The most literally of the Damtsig-pa
- [[Ranger]] - Similar source of power as druids.
- [[Warlock]] - Gain power through a contract with a deity.
