## Overview

A frozen region that appeared 800 years ago, destroying two major cities and cutting off land travel to the Orc and Goblin homelands. What caused its appearance has remained a mystery. In fact, very little at all is known about it for until 30 years ago it had been deemed impossible to enter or for anything to leave. That belief was shattered when the first [[Frost Orc Tribes]] made contact with the [[Goblin]]. Contact was quickly shut back down by the [[Frost Orc Tribes]].
