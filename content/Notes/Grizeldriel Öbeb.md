Inspired by Lucifer many of her names are direct allusions.  Started as a runt, became [[The Fallingstar]], felled a [[Cruentus|god]], took the mantle of the [[Dawnbringer]] and purposefully heralded in the [[Concursudio]].

## Background 
*Mythologized as both [[The Fallingstar]] and [[Dawnbringer]]*. Here is her actual story.
### Runt of the Litter
Born to the [[Last Queen - Z+T’s Mother|Last Queen]] of [[House Nga-khrulpa|Clan Nga-khrulpa]]; as the runt of the litter she was culturally destined to death. However, her [[Zelda + Tarmo’s Father|father]], a [[Elf]], took her to raise her with him among his family. While she is called *The First Veritkar*, technically that honor goes to [[Tarmé-mo|Tarmo]] her older sister by an hour and 8 minutes. Even older was a brother. (No link *wink wink*)
### Hexblade Prodigy
Raised as a [[Warlock#Hexblade]] of [[Cruentus]] led her father's armies against the [[Human|Humans]].  She was a brilliant general. It was in these battles she began experiments that would eventually developed into [[The Thrill]].  The destruction she left on the battlefield gained her the name [[The Fallingstar]].  

### The Tyranny of the Fallingstar
At about 120 years old she perfected [[The Thrill]] took it deep into human territory this sparked [[The Thrill#The Forgotten First War|The Forgotten First War]] leading to war amongst the [[Human|Humans]] and against the [[Goliath|Goliaths]]. 

With them preoccupied she was able to turn her sights towards the upstart [[House Tsönpal-me|Clan Tsönpal-me]]. Who had slaughter much of [[House Nga-khrulpa|Clan Nga-khrulpa]] including her mother and older brother, though given her past she didn't give much thought when it happened it provided an excellent justification to rule [[Regentseat]]. Which she was given armies by the [[Elf|Elves]] because [[Cruentus|Trakgö-pa]] had started  instigating the [[House Tsönpal-me|Tsönpal-me]] to attack the [[Elf|Elves.]]

After conquering [[Regentseat]] she marched her armies back to the lands of humans and goliaths leading several campaigns and beginning to conquer the north in the name of [[Cruentus]]. 

### Dawnbringer: Oscario
A [[Elf]] named [[Oscario]] begins to lead a quiet dissent among the verdikar, elves, and even humans about [[The Fallingstar]]'s never-ending wars and brutal rule. He is known as the [[Dawnbringer]] for bringing light to the [[Shadow of The Fallingstar]]. Unbeknownst of this [[Grizeldriel Öbeb|Grizelda]] invited, [[Oscario]] to her court as his fame as a [[Bard]] had spread far and wide. Given his chance he attempts to assassinate her. She effortlessly thwarts him and keeps him around as a a show of power for she does not fear this assassin. 

Overtime, as she has kept him close [[Oscario]] is able to eventually explain what [[Deorumors]] has said [[Cruentus]] and the implications of her continued conquering and his plans on the [[Mortal Realm]].  He begins to teach her some of the fundamentals of [[Den Chöton Magical Theory#Nüpé-tönpa]] to her as the formulate a plan on turning against [[Cruentus]]. 

### The Annihilation of [[Regentseat]]
She invited many [[Cruentus]] worshipers to [[Regentseat]] and then called a comet down from space with [[Oscario]] and [[Deorumors]]. Destroying [[Regentseat]], killing many of his believers and setting up the basis of the events [[Oscario]] would use to tell anyone who would listen that [[Cruentus]] was obviously a coward that had turned against his own followers. 

This was the start of [[War of The Felled Star]]. 

### Sealing of [[Cruentus]]
With the help of [[Deorumors]] and [[Schmiedevater|Resax Faber]]; she was able to lock [[Cruentus]].

### Never Again
The team devised a plot so that no deity would ever again try to do what [[Cruentus]] had tried.  Founding [[Deifractoria]] within [[Oscario|Oscario's]] [[The Narradei|Narradei]] and starting the [[War of the Dawn]]. These waged on for several hundred years but [[Imperium Jampyul|The Imperium]] was found around the time of [[Grizeldriel Öbeb|Grizelda's]] 200th birthday. The same year she found [[Academy Luminaria]] to train wizard soldiers for her armies and make sure that [[Regentseat]] would have a firm grasp on their technological edge. 

### Peaceful Rule and Mixing of Religions
The [[Concursudio]] occurs while she holds [[Yolsa]] together. Rules peacefully for 500 years. 

During this time see took many husbands among orc tribes to solidify her ties to their leadership and [[House Lux]] a robust crop of 31 children; 3 born previous to her rule. She founded her [[Dawnbringer's Jewels]] across [[Yolsa]] promoting the [[The Narradei]] while she didn't found them she was definitely a sponsor and developed the rules of conduct mission statement still followed by the [[Deifractoria]] to this day. 
### Death
Seems to have died widely loved by all in her sleep.
