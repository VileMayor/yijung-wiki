# Nüpé-tönpa

---
>Master of Power || Tibetan 
---

## Overview

Magic users that gain their power through understanding and then manipulating the [[Fundamental Energies]]. Technically, the [[Damtsig-pa]] manipulate it as well, just through an intermediary. They must utilize intent and often must understand what it is they are doing in order to work the magics they use. 

While both classes work the same [[Fundamental Energies]] they have different approaches.

---

## Classes

- [[Bard]] - Are all about the love of all creation. They do believe the world is a song and they want to sing it to all. They learn and teach. They use musical instruments a mental focus in their spell casting.
- [[Wizard]] - Knowledge is Power; Good Knowledge is Brutally Efficient Power
