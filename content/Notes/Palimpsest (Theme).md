# [[Palimpsest (Theme)|Palimpsest]]

---
 >a manuscript or piece of writing material on which the original writing has been effaced to make room for later writing but of which traces remain.
 >- Oxford Languages

 Introduced to this idea by [[Themes & Inspirations#[Palimpsest (podcast) - Wikipedia](https //en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palimpsest_(podcast))|Palimpsest (podcast)]] but will be using it differently in [[Salbé]]. This place where gods and their histories can change. It seems significant to keep in mind that there would be traces of the past left behind and specifically writing so while this is not exactly analogous I think it is thematic for the world I have built. The actions of the past even those that have faded from cultural memory, either naturally or those that were helped to fade from memory, will continue to show through to the present and shape it.
