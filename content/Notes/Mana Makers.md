##  Overview
Established by [[Oscario]]. A secret organization for alchemist; typically a [[Cleric]], [[Druid]], [[Bard]], or [[Wizard]]. Rarely a [[Warlock]] or [[Rogue]]. There symbol is a [[Tortle]] which they hide in the murals within their shops a nod to [[Oscario]]'s little brother. 

When [[Shellmo]] arrived his appearance caused quite the stir and when he wanted to join... well they thought it only preordained.

Work with both [[Academy Luminaria]] and [[Testudox University]] but most of its members go to [[Testudox University]] in fact this is so often ture that if one did know know the origin of the [[Mana Makers]] one might assume it started as a college fraternity. 

## History & Origin
Created by [[Oscario]] as a counterbalance to the more Machiavellian approach he foresaw [[Deifractoria]] taking, this secret order is built on acquiring knowledge, ethical distribution of knowledge, and community building. While they would keep their membership secret communities with members are more likely to have schools with good teachers, libraries, and access to better medicine and technology.
