# [[Calendar Lux]]

---

## Overview

Calendar used by the Jampyulian Empire. Starts with dedication of the rebuilt [[Regentseat]]. There are three seasons in the area around [[Regentseat]] each lasting three months long. Each month has 4 weeks of 9 days. 

[[Rubiphal Drolma]] - Orbits every 9 days
[[Mukha Dekyi]] - Orbits every 36 days
