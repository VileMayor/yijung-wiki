## Adventure Premise:

A deal between the Goblins and the Dwarves to trade a newly developed method for refining mithril. The deal is set to take place at the palace of the Imperatrix Lux.

Parties Involved:

The Dwarven Ambassadors: Represented by the Tifenheim Ambassador. They want the players to guard the secret to ensure the deal goes through.

A Mysterious Patron: Secretly working for the Imperatrix Lux. She wants the players to steal the plans and make it obvious, causing distrust between the Goblins and Dwarves. This also keeps the plans out of the hands of The Gilded Fist.

The Goblins' Role: The goblins invented the refinement process, not the dwarves. They are motivated to make money and get their people into dwarf cities.

The Stakes: The information provides a new source for mithril, making it less rare. The waste product is also incredibly useful, a fact unknown to all parties.

Player Choices & Consequences: The players will be presented with two competing contracts by their handler, Ghost.

Path 1: With the Dwarven Ambassadors

Hook: The players receive the contract from the guild. Their first step is to track down where The Gilded Fist is operating. Ghost will point them toward a known associate who can be found at a tavern.

Encounter 1: The Gilded Fist Associate

Objective: Acquire the location of The Gilded Fist's hideout and the time Aemor Chiselbeard will be leaving.

NPC: Bregnor, a brash dwarf with a low-level membership in The Gilded Fist. He's at the tavern to brag about a big score and his connection to the "Four Fingers."

Mechanics: The encounter begins as soon as the players approach him. The first player must succeed on a DC 12 Persuasion or Deception check to get him to talk. Once at the table, they can make a DC 16 Persuasion check or a DC 10 Intimidation check to get the information. A successful Intimidation check will get the information but will cause Bregnor to tip off Aemor. Either way, they discover a magical key is being made by a human blacksmith named Greg Wilson. Aemor holds the deed to Greg's smithy as collateral for a loan.

A new objective is presented: to prevent the key from ending up in Aemor's hands. If the players take too long to act, the key will have already been stolen by a rival adventuring guild.

Encounter 2: Tailing Aemor Chiselbeard

Objective: Follow Aemor without being detected from The Gilded Fist hideout to Greg Wilson's smithy.

Mechanics: This is a series of three timed skill challenges. The Crowded Market (DC 14 Stealth), The Vertical Climb (DC 14 Athletics or Acrobatics), and The Lookout (DC 15 Stealth or Perception). One or two failures mean Aemor is aware he is being followed. Three failures mean the players lose him entirely.

Encounter 3: The Smithy of Greg Wilson

Objective: Secure the magical key or the deed to the smithy before Aemor Chiselbeard does.

Mechanics: The players can either Negotiate with Greg (DC 15 Deception/Persuasion check to gain his trust and offer to pay his debt) or Infiltrate the Smithy (DC 15 Stealth, DC 12 Investigation, and DC 14 Sleight of Hand). If Aemor arrives while they are inside, a combat encounter will start.

Confrontation: The players can confront Aemor and two Gilded Fist Thugs when they arrive. Aemor will fight, but he will flee if he takes more than half of his total hit points in damage (40 HP) or if a majority of his thugs are incapacitated. If he escapes, he will report back to his father, Keegan Chiselbeard, and The Gilded Fist will be prepared for the final confrontation.

Path 2: With the Mysterious Patron

Hook: Ghost immediately informs the players about the magical key and where and when to steal it.

Details: The players must steal the key before the Gilded Fist can show up. If they run into combat with the Gilded Fist, the contract will still be complete, but the story beat will change because now there are rumors that some other party was interfering.

**

Encounter 1: The Gilded Fist Associate

Objective: Acquire the location of The Gilded Fist's hideout and the time Aemor Chiselbeard will be leaving.

NPC: Bregnor, a brash dwarf with a low-level membership in The Gilded Fist. He's at the tavern to brag about a big score and his connection to the "Four Fingers." He's easily flattered but quick to get suspicious.

Mechanics: The encounter begins as soon as the players approach him. The first player to approach must make a Persuasion or Deception check (DC 12). If they succeed, he agrees to talk. If they fail, he becomes suspicious and dismisses them. A second attempt can be made, but the DC is now 18. A second failure causes him to leave the tavern. Once Bregnor is at the table, the players can attempt to extract the information by making a Persuasion (DC 16) or Intimidation (DC 10) check. A successful Intimidation check will tip off Aemor.

Encounter 2: Tailing Aemor Chiselbeard

Objective: Follow Aemor without being detected from The Gilded Fist hideout to Greg Wilson's smithy.

The Setting: The pursuit takes place on the narrow, crowded, and multilevel streets of the dwarven district. Aemor, being paranoid, will take a complex, weaving path with several chokepoints.

Mechanics: This is a series of three timed skill challenges.

1. The Crowded Market (DC 14 Stealth check)
    
2. The Vertical Climb (DC 14 Athletics or Acrobatics check)
    
3. The Lookout (DC 15 Stealth or Perception check)  
    One or two failures mean Aemor is now aware that someone is following him. Three failures mean the players lose him entirely.
    

Encounter 3: The Smithy of Greg Wilson

Objective: Secure the magical key or the deed to the smithy before Aemor Chiselbeard does.

The Setting: Greg's smithy is a cluttered, messy workshop. The deed is locked in a small strongbox in his office, and the uncompleted key is on his workbench.

NPC: Greg Wilson, a human blacksmith in his late 40s. He's proud of his craftsmanship but is financially ruined. Aemor and a few of his toughs will be arriving to collect the key.

Choices & Mechanics:

- Negotiate with Greg: Make a Deception/Persuasion Check (DC 15) to gain his trust. If successful, he'll tell them about his debt, and they can offer to pay it.
    
- Infiltrate the Smithy: Make a Stealth (DC 15), Investigation (DC 12), and Sleight of Hand (DC 14) check to get in and steal the deed or key. If Aemor arrives while they are inside, a combat encounter will start.
    
- Confrontation: The players can confront Aemor and his thugs when they arrive. Aemor will fight but will not fight to the death. The moment he takes more than half of his total hit points in damage (40 HP) or if a majority of his accompanying thugs are incapacitated, he will attempt to flee. If he escapes, he will report back to his father, and The Gilded Fist will be prepared for the final confrontation.
