## Overview
At the border between the magical phenomena causing the [[Frostlands]] is an incredible and dangerous wall. 

## Description
It extends high into the air with dark blue and snow covered sharp shards of ice extend from this wall blocking off all travel to and from the [[Frostlands]]. 

## Encounters / Challenges
### *Unclimbable Surface* 
Climbing the wall requires a **DC 35 STR (athletics) check**. On a failure, the character makes no progress.
### *Absolute Zero Surface*
A creature that touches the wall in anyway, takes **4d10 cold damage** and must succeed a **DC 18 CON saving throw** or game **1d4 stacks** of *[[The Unceasing Chill#Other Causes of Frostbite|Frostbite]]*. 

Any non-magical item that touches the wall is instantly destroyed. 

### *Self-Repairing* 
Wall has an **AC 18**  and a **Damage Thresold 20**. Any section that is destoryed is instantly regereated the next round. If any object or creature is within the hole treat as if they are touching the surface.
