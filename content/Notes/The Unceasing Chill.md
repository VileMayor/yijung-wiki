# The Unceasing Chill

> [!info]
> - Location: Within and surrounding the [[Frostlands]]
> - Effect: Builds stacks until you are transformed into a mindless creature of cold.
---
>The Unceasing Chill || Tibetan   
---

## Overview

The magical cold of the Frostlands will overtime transform anyone unprotected into a [[Rimebeast]].

---

## In-Game Effect

### Gaining *Frostbite*

While in the [[Frostlands]] outside of a source of magical heat either a [[Mé Khok|Fire-Heart]] or the aura of the [[Firekeeper]]. 

A creature must make a ***DC 12 CON* saving throw** every hour. On a failed save, the player gains a stack of *Frostbite*. If a creature reaches a 10 stack of *Frostbite* they are transformed into the equivalent [[Rimebeast]]. 

#### Other Causes of Frostbite

Some [[Rimebeast]] have abilities that cause *Frostbite* as well.

### *Frostbite* Recovery

A character within aura of a magical heat source can remove up to 2 stacks of Frostbite after an hour of **rest**. 

[[Frostbloom]] can be found rarely. Will cure 5 stacks of *Frostbite*.
