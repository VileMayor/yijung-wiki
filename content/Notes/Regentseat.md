## Overview
The capital of the [[Imperium Jampyul]]. The nexus of knowledge on Yolsa. 
## Description
Regentseat is an ancient marble-hewn city set into the foot of a mountain. With massive structures and rich engraving, statues, squares, markets, and filled with color. It feels distinctly under capacity though and some areas have fallen into slums and crime.

## History / Origin
### Forgotten Goblin Origins
Originally a home to the archaic [[Goblin|Goblins]] that would eventually found [[Ur Ur Nesa]]. These goblins were always more independent from the [[Orcs]] than most of their kin. 
### The Elven Creep
Although the [[Elven Arrival]] occurred close to [[Mount Gemmarca]] the [[Elf|Elves]] eventually expanded in all directions. This place was always of particular interest due to the unique varieties of plants and animals as well as minerals, ores, and jewels. 

They eventually pushed the [[Goblin|Ur Ur]] from this place and made it their own. 
### Birth of the Verdikar
The Orcs took over Regentseat and [[Cruentus]] fell for them in this time. With him [[Elf|Elves]] came to breed with [[Orc|Orcs]] creating the early hybrids that would become a species known as the [[Verdikar]]. This time lasted for about 400 hundred years. 

### Betrayal
[[Cruentus]] as [[Cruentus|Trakgö-pa]] secretly led a [[Orc House - Lux Rival]] to take over Regentseat from [[House Nga-khrulpa|Clan Nga-khrulpa]].
