## Buddhism

- Another overbearing influence. Impossible to tease out all the individual lines but the biggest ones are as follows. 
- [[Creation of Yijung]] is inspired by teachings that the universe arises because of the compassion of the Buddhas to give beings an existence in which they can clarify their kleshas
- [[Lhachönomics]] is inspired by the fact that gods are just another class of sentient beings instead of creators of the universe.
