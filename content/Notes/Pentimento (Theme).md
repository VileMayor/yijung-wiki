# [[Pentimento (Theme)|Pentimento]]
---
>  a visible trace of earlier painting beneath a layer or layers of paint on a canvas.
 >- Oxford Languages
 
Another reference to the idea of history showing in the present but more center on the idea of the way that we "paint" the past with legends and the truth of what happens still will shine through. This idea is admittedly also influenced by [[Themes & Inspirations#Brandon Sanderson|Sanderson's Stormlight Archive]]. I want many quests to lean on this idea that what is shown to you is an illusion and you need to pay close attention to see the through beneath the layers of bullshit painted on top.
