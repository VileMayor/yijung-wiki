# Mé Khok

Wondrous Item, Rare

This small, floating amorphous lump of dull grey glass or obsidian. Glows with a red light in side. Magical Devices used by the [[Frost Orc Tribes]] to ward off the [[The Unceasing Chill|The Unceasing Chill]].
