##  Overview
 A dwarven crime syndicate based in [[Reagentseat]] with operations across [[Yolsa]]. Their primary motives are gaining power in the edges of [[Imperium Jampyul|The Imperium]] and money.  They have a pet project to access the two [[Dawnbringer's Jewels]] that were lost when the [[Creation of the Frostlands|Frostlands appeared]]. This project is a source of internal conflict as it is overseen by a [[Grimgier Goldstahl's Nephew]] and is seen as a waste of resources by other members. 

## History & Origin
Started in [[Tifenheim]] but [[Schmiedevater]] drove them out.
