## Overview

## Etymology & Inspiration 
Means mind-born. Since it arises from the mind of [[Khamdak Gyurmé]].

Inspired by Buddhist Cosmology that Buddha’s manifest the physical world so that our minds may have forms and conditions to overcome our ignorance.
