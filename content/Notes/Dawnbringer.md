## Overview
Actually a conflation of two historic figures, [[Oscario]] and [[Grizeldriel Öbeb]]. Though [[Oscario]] was the first to hold the title, truly it is the memory of [[Grizeldriel Öbeb]]. She is beloved mother of [[Imperium Jampyul|The Imperium]].  [[Oscario]] was a large part of the reason [[Grizeldriel Öbeb|Grizelda]] was known by the title having spread the tales himself. 
## Stylized Appearance
A tall and abnormally muscular [[Verdikar]] woman with slightly more [[Elf|Elvish]] features than one would expect. Her hair is worn in intricate battle-braids down her back. She wears a simple dress that covers only the left breast and shoulder with deep slits on the sides; so that between her legs is like a flap and then a second flap cover her butt and wraps around the hips. Beneath she wears simple trousers and boots that go up her calves. She is always shown with a halo that is often the result of her axe blade is a massive moon shape being behind her head. In representation's that this is not the case, the axe is still present and she will have a halo of light instead. 

One common pose shows her with the haft of a massive axe  resting on her right shoulder with a the blade forming the halo. With a flexed arm she grips the haft showing off her massive arms. 

Most of her appearance is meant to emphasize her battle prowess but her breast is exposed as a reminder of her nurturing nature. 

She is a muscle mommy essentially.
