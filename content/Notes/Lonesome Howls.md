## Overview

**

The Threat: A newly turned, pregnant halfling werewolf.

Werewolf Lore in Salbé: Lycanthropy in this world is a magical disease tied to an ancient goliath god, Lai’Kon; who was likely the brother of their main deity. Lai’Kon had traveled back and forth between the physical and god realms before the Dawnbringer’s actions caused the Deocursus. During which he became stuck on the physical realm. Separated from his family he became lonely and in his isolation began to lash out at those he came across. Eventually he began the spread of lycanthropy on Salbé. Werewolves in Salbé do not turn in relation to the moon but in regard to hunger. The newly turned have little control in their wolf forms, though those strongly opposed to harming others seem to go after animals until the hunger grows. The hunger is worsened by a need for connection and can be satisfied by a special rite/feast and connection with a pack. The werewolf's wolf form is a large wolf the size of a horse.
