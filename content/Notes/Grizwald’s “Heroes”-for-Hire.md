## Overview

Major Adventuring Guild in [[Regentseat]]. Scouted the [[Players]] as a team at the beginning of our campaign. 


The guild has a well-recognized reputation among the public, and many of its teams have become local heroes with songs and tales told of their adventures. However, it is fiercely competitive with other guilds. Its primary rivals are Pinkerton’s Agents-to-Contract (criminal, prone to breaking rules) and The Guardian Angels (a lawful, "goody two shoes" guild run by a paladin that specializes in bodyguard work). Grizwald's guild has a neutral alignment, with a focus on promoting excellence as a public-facing image. The guild itself has an unspoken code, but the players will be left to decide how their own team approaches contracts.

Main Base of Operations is [[The Prudish Mermaid]]

## Structure & Leadership

Grizwald owns 7 taverns within [[Regentseat]] and uses them as bases of operation. He lets teams operate more independently out of several of them. This is not necessarily open knowledge. These serve as profit generators, as sources for stirring up leads for contracts, and as small bases for teams to operate out of. The independent taverns are all specialized in particular contracts.
