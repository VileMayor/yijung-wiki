# Tortle

---

## Overview

A species of humanoid turtles from [[Sam's Plane]].  They are not native to [[Salbé|Salbé]] however two have made it their. [[Oscario|One]] spiritually, the [[Shellmo|other]] physically.  

[[Oscario]] made the [[Tortle]] a known symbol in the orders he (co)-founded, [[The Narradei]] and [[Mana Makers]]. [[Deorumors]] is often shown with a turtle in reference to [[Oscario|Oscario's]], her lovers, former body.
