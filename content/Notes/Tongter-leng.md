## Overview

in some places cold air collects dangerously. 

## In-Game Effect

### *Lethal Cold Gale*

Upon entering the area of effect and then which round within:
- Take **10d10 cold damage**
- Make a **DC 20 STR saving throw**, on a failed save get pushed 60 ft and knocked prone. 
- Gain **1d4 stacks** of *[[The Unceasing Chill#Other Causes of Frostbite|Frostbite]]*
