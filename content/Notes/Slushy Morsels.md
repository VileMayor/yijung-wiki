## Notes

Player Choices & Consequences: The players are offered a choice at the start of the quest that determines the entire nature of the adventure.

Path 1: With a Guide (Hire Chésung)

Cost: 400 gp.

Details: With Chésung's intimate knowledge of the Frostlands, the players will not have to make Nature checks to navigate. He can expertly guide them to the specific locations for each sample, making the journey a narrative progression rather than a survival challenge. The Mé Khok in Chésung's possession is stable and will not sputter or break down. A character can remove up to 2 stacks of Frostbite for every hour of resting in the aura of the working Mé Khok.

Risk: The primary danger is being spotted by the other Frost Orc clans. Every two hours of travel, the party will make a group Stealth check with an escalating DC. On a failure, they must roll on the following table:

1-2: A lone Frost Orc Raider spots the party. Upon seeing the outsiders, the Raider immediately flees to inform their clan of the broken treaty. This sets a timer for a future confrontation.

3-4: A patrol from Chésung's own clan has found the party. This will lead to a tense social encounter where Chésung must convince his allies to let the outsiders pass without a fight.

5: A friend of Chésung's has found them. This is an opportunity for a roleplaying moment, and the scout might provide a small bit of aid, like a tip or a few rations.

6: The party has been spotted by a high-level patrol, led by a powerful Frost Orc Druid. This will lead to a very difficult combat encounter designed for a higher-level party.

Path 2: Without a Guide (Go It Alone)

Reward: The full 800 gp.

Details: The players must rely on rumors and make Nature checks to find the sample locations (DC 12 for the Rimedeer, DC 15 for the flora, and DC 18 for the ice core).

Mé Khok: The Mé Khok they possess has an increasing chance of breaking down. A d20 roll of 3-4 causes it to sputter; a creature with proficiency in Arcana or Tinker's Tools can make a DC 12 check to stabilize it.

Environmental Cold: When the Mé Khok fails, the players will have to endure the environmental cold. They must make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw every hour of travel. A failed save results in gaining one stack of Frostbite. If a creature accumulates 10 stacks of Frostbite, they are immediately transformed into a Rimebeast. If the party is in danger of a TPK, Chésung will appear to save them
