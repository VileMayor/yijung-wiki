>Laws of the Shifting Gods || Tibetolatin

## Overview
The mechanics of how the gods change with the belief of their followers. Their personality, powers, histories, and even relationships and the shape of their worlds can change as their followers beliefs change. 

They stories of their gods are more stable than in our world because the gods do interact directly with followers. 

## Mechanics
Are at their strongest when believers believe them to be all powerful and the only holder of such power. 

As followers believer otherwise their power grows weaker. [[Den Chöton Magical Theory]]

They are much more powerful in their home domains within the [[God Realm]]. Most domains connect with a specific place on [[Salbé|Salbé]] that acts a a point of crossing from one to the other.

While on [[Salbé|Salbé]] proper, they still experience fluctuations in power as they normally would but their personalities are more stable. They can not die while on [[Salbé|Salbé]] but their forms can be destroyed forcing them to return to the [[God Realm]]. and their personality to align if it has shifted. 

The [[God Realm]] itself is shaped by believers. Mutually known gods lands are combined *forcefully* causing mountain rnages to rise up when this occurs.
