## Overview
From [[Sam's Plane]], he is [[Shellmo|Shellmo's]] older brother. He has become wrapped up in fate of [[Salbé|Salbé]] and the lover of [[Deorumors]].  He convinced [[Grizeldriel Öbeb|Grizelda]] to turn against [[Cruentus]] and found with her the [[Deifractoria]] and by himself [[The Narradei]] and [[Mana Makers]]. He currently resides in a [[Lentomthol|Time Pocket]] stuck there since [[Creation of the Frostlands]]. He sent for his [[Shellmo|brother]] in hopes of rescue, setting up the series of events that transferred him to [[Salbé|Salbé]] as well.

## Background
### Before His "Death"
[[Oscario]] was born [[Tortle]] in [[Sam's Plane]]. He is the older brother of [[Shellmo]]. He is an alchemical prodigy and was experimenting with travel to other planes. His experiments caught the attention of the wrong people and he was killed on the night before [[Shellmo]] became 15.

### A Disembodied Spirit
Luckily, he was testing a new version of his elixir when he was murdered and instead of dying the tie to his body was severed. Desperate to get back to his brother and finding there is little he could do as a spirit, he entered a divinatory trance. However, in this plane, [[Fae Plane]], his ability was hampered by not having the gods he was used to calling upon.

### A Chance Encounter
[[Oscario]] has been trancing for years and one day his thoughts, his meditations, caught the attention of [[Deorumors]]. He fascinated her with his stories broke her heart with how badly he wanted to make it back to his brother. and she fascinates him with her knowledge and possibly a chance to learn how to go back. She takes pity on his predicament and arranges for him to take up residence in the body of a brain-dead [[Elf]] musician of considerable skill.
### New Body, New Love, New Horizons
Waking up in a new body on [[Salbé]], a place so different from where he was from. Most of his knowledge of magical working from [[Sam's Plane]] was useless to him, but [[potion craft]] was basically the same; while the ingredients and reagents were different his potion-making techniques stayed true. He lost much but he also gained. His new body was able to play many instruments and had remarkable control of its rich voice. He now had an intrinsic knack for turns of phrase and lyrical intuition; not to mention having had no need to learn [[Elvish Antiquivox|Antiquivox]] as the body already knew. 

The first 3 or so years on [[Salbé]] he studied with [[Deorumors]] who had taken to calling him 'Her [[Oscario|Testudox]]' as the fell in love. He gifted her a Mithril Turtle Pendant that was inscribed with sentiments of love. As their love grew so did his knowledge and power. They traveled the [[Elf|Elvish]] and [[Orc|Orcish]] lands and she taught him lessons given in public where he would softly play music and listen. Sometimes crowds would gather sometimes they would be in an empty square. 
### Roots of [[The Narradei]] and the [[Concursudio]]
[[Deorumors]] learns about the creation of [[The Thrill]] and reluctantly separates from [[Oscario]] not wanting him to be anywhere near the war brewing around it. He begins to explore other lands meeting with as many different peoples of he falls in love with their stories and begins to tell them as he travels for free lodging and food. He hopes to find magics that will allow him to travel to other planes as each of these gods he finds people worshipping seems to grant different magics. 

No one had ever done this before and it brought changes to the world of the gods. The collisions of [[God Lands]] that had been close but unconnected. His tales hadn't spread that far but it was enough that [[Deorumors]] who was already seeking answers as to what had happened to her brother was able to piece together the beginnings of [[Lhachönomics]] and [[Den Chöton Magical Theory]]. 
### The Dawnbringer - Building [[The Narradei]]
Having realized [[Cruentus]] has no intention of changing course [[Deorumors]] urges her [[Oscario|Testudox]] to action. He spreads stories of the old [[Cruentus]] and of peace. He speaks against [[Grizeldriel Öbeb]] and her constant wars. But he also tells every other tale he knows. He begins to travel the budding [[Imperium Jampyul]] gaining the name [[Dawnbringer]] for bringing light to the [[Shadow of The Fallingstar]].

He begins an order he calls [[The Narradei]] and encourages others to do as he does. Spreading hope and raising people in rebellion ready to stand again [[The Fallingstar]]. This probably went on four or so years. 

Eventually famous enough to catch [[Grizeldriel Öbeb|Grizelda's]] attention and she calls for him to appear in her court. He does his normal performance including his denunciations of her and then attempts to assassinate her. 

He honestly stood no chance and she keeps him as a her court jester. Where he works to talk sense into her. Exposing [[Cruentus]]' plan to come to the [[Mortal Realm]]. 
### Shaken Faith - Buds of the [[Deifractoria]]
Horrified by the plan formulated by [[Grizeldriel Öbeb|Grizelda]] and [[Deorumors]] to destroy [[Regentseat]]. [[Oscario]] decides he can't be a part of this even though he can't fully condemn their actions. He decides he can help in another way returns to his travels and now spreading propaganda he developed with [[Deorumors]] to weaken [[Cruentus]] and maybe find gods that will ally with them against [[Cruentus]].

The dragging [[War of The Felled Star]] ensues.

### **EMPIRE**
Following the [[Sealing of Cruentus]]; [[Deorumors]] and [[Grizeldriel Öbeb|Grizelda]] come up with a plan so that it never happens again the [[Imperium Jampyul]]. A religious empire that would spread the belief of all gods so know could attempt what [[Cruentus]] had again. Grizelda started the [[War of the Dawn]] to forge this dream.

Between this and his objections over the genocide at [[Regentseat]]; he ends his relationship with [[Deorumors]]. He still understands the importance of their work and continues to build up [[The Narradei]] and even works with [[Grizeldriel Öbeb|Grizelda]] to form the [[Deifractoria]] as a shadow organization within.  At this point it was a council with the command to develop the plan of attack for spreading the beliefs of the gods. Which ones to break first and yada yada to make sure the war progressed in [[Imperium Jampyul|The Imperium's]] favor. 

### [[Mana Makers]] - A Small Resistance 
While he saw in the importance he worried about them overlooking the people and possibly utilizing Machiavellian means so as a counterbalance he created the [[Mana Makers]] separate from the [[The Narradei]]. They were both a tribute to his home and the organization that could claim to be the reason his [[Shellmo|brother's]] and his own life wasn't spent starving in a street. To honor his brother he made the [[Tortle]], a creature unknown to this world, its secret symbol. Think Christians and the fish; alchemist who are part of the order will have a [[Tortle]] 'hidden'. DC 14 WIS (perception) in a mural in their store. Their murals alone are almost a give away as each has a lovely mural depicting a local legendary mystery but other have taken up the murals as well without knowing about the hidden nod to [[Shellmo]]. 

### [[Testudox University]]
As [[Imperium Jampyul|The Imperium]] began to coalesce into its final form and his organizations became big enough to run without him [[Oscario]] took a step back and realized how sidetracked he had become from returning to [[Sam's Plane]] he began to study again in [[Regentseat]] even collaborating with [[Deorumors]] from time to time. After a few years of slow progress and realizing the wealth and influence he had access to as well as seeing an opportunity to balance his friend [[Grizeldriel Öbeb|Grizelda's]] approach to power at by the [[Wizard|Wizards]] at [[Academy Luminaria]]. 

Establishing [[Testudox University]] with its many colleges and disciplines for [[Bard|Bards]] they would also work magic through the manipulation of the [[Fundamental Energies]] but they would extol its beauty and use art as the focus of their spells.  While [[Academy Luminaria]] was to be solely dedicated to how to gain power through magic and technology(applied sciences); even if it does explore all the schools of it. [[Testudox University]] would also study people, science and art as well. 

He set up one whole College to investigate his problems with interplanar travel to see if he could return to his brother or communicate with him. 

### Decades of Entrenchment
Breakthroughs on his problem were slow and those that did occur were slow. He set back out to his travels again devoting much of his time to [[The Narradei]] while initiating more [[Mana Makers]] all over the [[Imperium Jampyul]]. He did this for maybe 120 years.

During this time he was in part guided by tracking down [[Schmiedevater|Resax Faber's]] [[Divine Forge Weapons]] and establishing colleges in all many of the [[Dawnbringer's Jewels]].
### Break Through
The University in [[Domusargha]] found there was a thin spot the planar walls in the area. [[Oscario]] spent the next 10 years working on his [[Lentomthol|Time Pocket]] which he found but then set up for habitation. When ready he entered and told his assistants to call on him in 108 years. 
### Time Flies By
After the first he told [[Grizeldriel Öbeb|Grizelda]] of his discovery and promised to continue to guide the [[Deifractoria]] coming back often enough and for long enough to keep them on track. Overtime he found that it varied some but about three day inside passed for each *year* outside. And he developed a routine of 108 years inside and 1 year outside. He would experience this as approximately equal 6 month alternating blocks. About 30 years have passed for him by the time our campaign starts. 

While inside he would study the most recent developments from his colleges and the intelligence gathered from the three orders he overlocked. Real advances start to take place at his university in [[Domusargha]] and when they slowed he would alternate more frequently to help breakthrough roadblocks. He was able to guide his secret societies. He began to focus more and more on his divinatory abilities and continuing the experimentation for interplanar magics inside the [[Lentomthol|Time Pocket]]. 

He began to have dreams of being stuck in the pocket as plane after plane was born and ended. This filled him with worry for [[Salbé|Salbé]] as his [[The Narradei]] had begun to pick up tales from another continent that worried him. Tales of continents ruled by a united singular pantheon. Not each with their own. Half the world may all worship the same gods. This caused him to develop a plan to summon his brother forth from [[Sam's Plane]] to rescue him just incase the need ever occurred.  They had also been able to locate [[Sam's Plane]] and knew that time passed at rate of a similar magnitude as the [[Lentomthol|Time Pocket]] and that it would be easier than rejoining Shellmo himself. So his brother was likely alive and maybe able to save [[Yolsa]]. He began to lay stories with in the [[Mana Makers]] with details he had divined about his brother. So that if he was ever able to bring him and he was unable to meet up with Shellmo he would find himself taken care of by any member of the order and quickly made the leader if his skill was what his brother believed it would be. 

### Time Stops - A Call for Help
Several stops before the [[Creation of the Frostlands]] they developed a way to communicate with [[Oscario]] and a way to teleport people from certain other realms to [[Den Chöton|Den Chöton]].  The time delay made it painful but it could be done to communicate extremely important information. Used to communicate updates to the Time Pocket, Oscario mostly communicated back with predetermined code so that more information could be communicated with a single symbol. He put even more plans inplace to summon his brother for aid if the need arose setting up basically a dead mans switch. 

He noticed the lack of updates pretty quickly and his divinatory senses confirmed. Has nothing to do now but hope that his brother will find him soon. As he tries to divine all that he can about the [[Threat from Across the Seas]]
