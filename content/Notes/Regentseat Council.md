## Overview
The real power in [[Imperium Jampyul|The Imperium]] since [[Creation of the Frostlands]] the [[Imperatrix Lux]] as an institution has lost a lot of power to the council because there isn't much empire any more. 
## Seated Houses
There are nine seats on the council.  Seats belong to the house's and who sits in them up to each house to determine. 
1. [[House Lux]] - 4th Tier - Sat by someone within the house other the the imperatrix. Usually appointed by the Imperatrix. Sometimes, the retired imperatrix. 
2. [[Elven House - Lux Ally]] - 3rd Tier E
3. [[Verdikar House - Lux Ambi]] - 3rd Tier W
4. [[House Tsönpal-me]] -  2nd Tier E
5. [[House Aemulus]] - 2nd Tier C ** Owns the lands related to werewolf quest
6. [[Dwarf House - Lux Ally]] -  2nd Tier W
7. [[Human House - Lux Rival]] - 1st Tier E
8. [[House Nga-khrulpa]] - 1st Tier C
9. [[Orc House - Lux Allyl]] - 1st Tier W

## History
Established at the beginning of [[Imperium Jampyul|The Imperium]] its membership has historically been very stable. This has not been true recently with changes such as the union of a human house with Aemulus and replacement of one failed human house with that of a dwarven house.
