## Overview

The procurement agent for the Dawnbringer's Academy, Zelda is a 400-year-old half-Elf/Verdikar. She is extremely competent at her job, blunt, and short-tempered. She enjoys her work and has a vast network of contacts, some of whom are "unsavory." Her close relationship with her half-brother, Grizwald, is a soft spot.
