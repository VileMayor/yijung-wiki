## Overview

A former druid apprentice, Kharlo quit his training to care for his younger siblings after his mother died from an illness. He began working with the Gilded Fist to acquire medicine for one of his siblings who has developed the same illness, which is a common, mundane ailment with remedies available in Regentseat. The players could get involved in helping him.
